# Malaysia boundary profile

The `my` profile applies the common baseline plus Malaysia strict terms and labelled MyKad/MyPR and 12-digit SSM
registration patterns. It routes CMSA sections 188-189 as MNPI and MyKad/PDPA Malaysia terms as PII signals. It is a
lexical boundary profile, not a legal decision.

## Research

- [PDPA and cross-border legal advisory work](./pdpa.md)

## Citation convention

Prefer the Personal Data Protection Commissioner's current Act 709 collection, official gazette/legislation records,
and SSM records for entity identifiers. Record issuer, title, version/date, official URL, access date, and the exact
provision relied upon.

## Known limits

The profile cannot establish Act 709 scope, a section 129 transfer condition, role allocation, consent/notice validity,
or whether a business identifier is personal data in the matter context.
