# Code Of Conduct Enforcement

Shisa adopts Contributor Covenant 2.1 in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

## Enforcement Chain

1. Intake: reports go to the project contact method once configured.
2. Triage: at least two maintainers review whether immediate moderation is needed.
3. Conflict check: any maintainer directly involved in the report recuses themself.
4. Investigation: maintainers collect relevant public links, timestamps, screenshots, and reporter-provided context.
5. Decision: maintainers choose the Contributor Covenant impact level and consequence.
6. Notice: affected parties receive the decision, consequence, duration, and appeal path.
7. Record: public bans are recorded publicly; private reporter details stay private.
8. Appeal: appeals are reviewed by maintainers not involved in the original decision when possible.

## Current Gaps

The project email alias and private appeals tracker are not configured yet. Until they are configured, enforcement docs must not claim a working private intake channel.

## Records

Keep private records minimal:

- report date
- people involved
- links or evidence
- decision
- consequence
- appeal status

Do not store unrelated personal data.
