# Launch Post Draft

Every law firm checks whether a published case is still good law. Nobody reliably checks whether the
firm's own knowledge still is.

Solomon applies good-law reasoning to internal positions, precedents, clauses, and house views. It stores
firm knowledge bi-temporally, tracks what each item depends on, and flags re-verification when an authority
or internal dependency moves. It does this behind the Solomon boundary: context is reviewed, pseudonymized, and demasked
through a boundary designed for zero-retention workflows.

This is not another legal search box. A search box finds the 2023 memo. Solomon explains that the memo
depends on Regulation R section 12, that section changed in 2025, and the memo has not been re-verified.
The answer is not "wrong"; it is stale-pending-human-review.
