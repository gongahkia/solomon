# Singapore canonical authority sources

Checked: 2026-07-12. Use this routing note to identify an external authority source; it does not determine legal
effect, commencement, interpretation, or whether an item should be marked stale.

## Source hierarchy

| Need | Primary source | Use in Solomon | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gazetted legislation, subsidiary legislation, notifications, and notices | [Singapore Electronic Gazette](https://www.egazette.gov.sg/) | Capture publication URL, title/number, Gazette date/time, supplement, and retrieved hash as authority-change evidence. | The eGazette says it is the prescribed website under Interpretation Act s 48; gazettes published there on or after 9 March 2025 are prima facie evidence and prevail over other forms. |
| Current/consolidated legislation, historical versions, revised editions, and published supplements | [Singapore Statutes Online](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/) | Locate an authority, compare versions, and link an internal dependency to title/provision/version. | SSO's [About page](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/About-us) says its reproduced legislation is unofficial and should not be relied on as authoritative text. Confirm publication/effect through the appropriate Gazette or legal review. |
| Legislative publication mechanics | [Electronic Gazette and Legislation Act 2025](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Acts-Supp/5-2025/Published/20250211?DocDate=20250211) | Record why a published eGazette item is the canonical event source for post-commencement updates. | Check the provision's commencement and the actual Gazette item; do not infer that every SSO display is authoritative. |
| Draft bills and proposed amendments | [SSO Bills Supplement](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Bills-Supp/) | Store as pending-amendment or monitoring evidence, never as an enacted authority change. | A bill is not enacted law; attach status and publication date. |

## Recording rule

For a Singapore authority event, retain at least: canonical URL, authority title/number, provision/version where
available, Gazette publication date/time, retrieval timestamp, source hash, source classification, and human review
status. Register a change only after a curator confirms what moved and its relationship to internal positions.

## Do not infer

- A webpage update alone establishes commencement, applicability, or legal effect.
- A bill, consultation, press release, or secondary summary is an enacted authority change.
- A source link removes the need for lawyer interpretation or jurisdiction-specific advice.
