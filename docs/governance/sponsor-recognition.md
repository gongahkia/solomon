# Sponsor Recognition

Sponsor recognition is gratitude, not governance.

Allowed recognition:

- name or logo on a sponsor page after sponsor approval
- periodic thank-you notes in release posts
- aggregate funding summaries

Not allowed:

- roadmap priority in exchange for payment
- private security exceptions
- maintainer vote weight
- endorsement language implying Shisa recommends a sponsor product
- access to private reporter or security details

Maintainers may decline recognition that creates a conflict of interest or makes the project look vendor-controlled.
