# Naming

## Decision

The product name remains Decorum.

Do not rename it to "LinkedIn Community Notes," "Community Notes for LinkedIn," "X for LinkedIn," or any other name that puts LinkedIn, X, or Community Notes in the product name. "Decorum" is a standalone name, does not include a platform mark, and does not claim sponsorship.

No formal trademark clearance has been completed.

## Public Copy Rules

- Use "Decorum" as the product name.
- Describe the host platform as "for LinkedIn posts" only when needed to explain compatibility.
- Pair public compatibility mentions with "unaffiliated" or equivalent disclaimer language.
- Do not use LinkedIn or X logos in the extension UI, docs hero copy, store listings, repo badges, or screenshots unless written permission exists.
- Do not describe Decorum as "official," "certified," "partnered," "endorsed," "Community Notes," or "LinkedIn Community Notes."
- Do not copy LinkedIn or X product chrome, marks, or overall brand presentation in marketing surfaces.
- Keep Community Notes references limited to internal product comparison, visual research, and test fixture language.

## Current Approved Copy

- "Decorum"
- "Unaffiliated context cards for LinkedIn posts."
- "Decorum is an unaffiliated Manifest V3 browser extension prototype that adds restrained context cards under LinkedIn posts."

## Rationale

LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits stating or implying affiliation or endorsement without express consent. LinkedIn's Brand Guidelines also prohibit brand use that could confuse source, sponsorship, or affiliation, and say usage should remain separate from the LinkedIn brand without copying the platform look and feel.

X's brand toolkit directs users of X trademarks and resources to follow X Trademark Guidelines and related X policies. [Inference] Avoiding X and Community Notes in the product name reduces avoidable brand-association risk for Decorum's current private prototype.
