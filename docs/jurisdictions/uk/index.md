# United Kingdom boundary profile

The `uk` profile applies the common baseline plus UK strict terms and NI, labelled UTR, labelled Companies House, and
labelled NHS patterns. It validates labelled NHS numbers with Mod 11 and routes UK MAR/inside-information terms as
MNPI. It is a lexical boundary profile, not a legal decision.

## Research

- [SRA supervision and ICO AI/data-protection](./ai-data-protection.md)
- [EU AI Act legal-decision-support note](./eu-ai-act.md)

## Citation convention

Prefer current SRA, ICO, legislation.gov.uk, and other issuing-authority sources. Record issuer, title, version/date,
official URL, access date, and provision/guidance section; distinguish binding law, professional rules, and guidance.

## Known limits

The profile cannot decide UK GDPR applicability, lawful basis, Article 22 implications, adequate human supervision,
legal accuracy, or whether a financial fact is inside information.
