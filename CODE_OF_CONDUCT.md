# Code of Conduct

Close Enough is a professional, welcoming project. Participants must be respectful, constructive, and inclusive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment; discriminatory or demeaning language; threats; personal attacks; deliberate disruption; and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when conduct conflicts with these standards. Report an incident privately to angryapplegravy@gmail.com. Reports will be handled confidentially as far as practical and reviewed in good faith.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when representing Close Enough in public. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
