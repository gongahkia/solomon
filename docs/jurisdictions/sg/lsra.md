# Singapore LSRA and law-practice deployment boundary

Checked: 2026-07-12. This is a research note, not legal advice, licensing analysis, or a determination that Solomon's
deployment is permitted for any law practice entity.

## Primary sources

- [MinLaw: Legal Services Regulatory Authority](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/our-work/legal-services-regulatory-authority/)
- [MinLaw: LSRA legislation and communications](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/law-practice-entities-and-lawyers/resources-for-law-practice-entities/relevant-legislation-and-communications/)
- [Singapore Statutes Online: Legal Profession Act 1966](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/LPA1966)

MinLaw describes the LSRA as a Ministry of Law department led by the Director of Legal Services. It licenses or
registers law-practice entities, sets/reviews business criteria and licence conditions, oversees compliance, approves
specified collaborative arrangements, and registers foreign-qualified lawyers and regulated non-practitioners. The
current MinLaw resource page points to the Legal Profession Act, the Law Practice Entities Rules, Regulated Individuals
Rules, modified-application rules for international services, and Professional Conduct Rules.

The current Legal Profession Act includes a law-practice-entities part covering law firms, limited-liability law
partnerships, law corporations, Singapore group practices, Singapore-foreign law practice collaborations, and qualifying
foreign law practice structures. [Unverified] This review did not identify an AI-specific LSRA rule or licence condition;
do not infer that none exists or that general legal-profession rules do not apply.

## Deployment review

1. Identify the law-practice entity type, services, users, jurisdiction, and any foreign/collaborative arrangement.
2. Check the current LPA, applicable subsidiary legislation, LSRA licence/registration terms, and Law Society requirements.
3. Separately assess professional conduct, confidentiality, data protection, AI-vendor, client-consent, supervision, and insurance implications.
4. Retain deployment architecture, role/approval record, and sample currency audit evidence with the firm's legal review.

## Limits

deployment-specific evidence and qualified Singapore review.
