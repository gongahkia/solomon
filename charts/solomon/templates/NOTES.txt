Solomon is deployed with OIDC, PostgreSQL/pgvector, a migration hook, API, console, and worker.

Create the referenced Kubernetes Secrets before install. The chart never renders secret data.
Use `helm upgrade --install --wait` so the migration hook completes before the app resources are released.
