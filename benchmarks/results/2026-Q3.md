## Methodology

Stanford HAI legal hallucination benchmarks are useful methodology references for
legal-answer hallucination rates, but their tasks do not directly evaluate
firm-internal authority currency. [Inference] Solomon evaluates the translated
currency task: detect stale internal knowledge after authority supersession,
propagate through dependency edges, and avoid false stale flags.

Damien Charlotin stale-authority examples are curated in
`benchmarks/cases/stale-authority-cases.json` for stale-precedent and
repealed-law scenario design.
