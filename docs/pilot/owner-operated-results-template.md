## Owner-approved redacted summary checklist

Before committing a summary, confirm each item:

- [ ] no raw source, evidence spans, screenshots, client/matter names, personal identifiers, credentials, DSNs, or
      private paths;
- [ ] no claim of external validation, legal accuracy, production adoption, reviewer usability validation, or legal
      advice;
- [ ] the summary calls the run an owner-operated pilot / structured self-evaluation;
- [ ] measurements distinguish performed, not assessed, and not performed work;
- [ ] pre-registered expectations and observed results are described without reinterpreting them after the fact;
- [ ] the owner approved the redacted summary for repository publication.
