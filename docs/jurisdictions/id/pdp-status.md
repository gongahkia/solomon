# Indonesia PDP Law: source status

Checked: 2026-07-12. This is a source-status note, not legal advice, an implementation-status determination, or a
compliance assessment.

## Verified primary source

- [JDIH Kemkomdigi: Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/832/t/undangundang%2Bnomor%2B27%2Btahun%2B2022)
- [Official law download](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/unduh/id/832/t/undangundang%2Bnomor%2B27%2Btahun%2B2022)

The official JDIH record identifies Law No. 27 of 2022 as the Personal Data Protection Law and records its adoption
date as 17 October 2022. The official law text addresses personal-data protection, including protection from unlawful
access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, destruction, and loss.
