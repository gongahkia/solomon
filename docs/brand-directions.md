# Solomon brand directions

Status: exploratory marks for product review, not a selected identity or trademark clearance.

The marks avoid gavels, scales, coins, brains, chat bubbles, and conventional AI sparkles. Each is a hand-authored SVG with a 64 × 64 viewBox, no raster dependency, and a two-colour maximum. They are designed to retain their silhouette when rendered at 16 px and to work as a single-colour mark by assigning one `currentColor` value to every path.

## Direction 1 — Verified Thread

![Verified Thread](./assets/logo-directions/verified-thread.svg)

**Idea.** Three records are connected by one vertical thread. The thread reaches through a previously checked record and a current record: Solomon does not merely store knowledge; it keeps its relationship to verification visible.

**Palette.** Ink `#102A43`; verification lime `#D5F249`; monochrome `#102A43` or white.

**Type.** Use IBM Plex Sans, 600 weight, lower-case wordmark with 1% tracking. Its compact, technical drawing complements a structural mark without legal-firm serif cues.

**SVG-ready construction.** Two rounded horizontal record capsules, each 14 units high, begin at x=12. The upper capsule ends at x=44; the lower ends at x=59. A 6-unit lime vertical stroke at x=42 joins their centres. At small sizes the capsules read as two deliberate bars and the thread remains a distinct accent.

## Direction 2 — Continuity Fold

![Continuity Fold](./assets/logo-directions/continuity-fold.svg)

**Idea.** One uninterrupted folded path makes an S: a knowledge position may change state, but its provenance remains continuous. The warm endpoint signals the point that merits attention without using an alert triangle.

**Palette.** Charcoal `#18212F`; revision amber `#E4863A`; monochrome `#18212F` or white.

**Type.** Use Manrope, 650 weight, lower-case wordmark with normal tracking. Its rounded terminals echo the mark’s turns while staying restrained.

**SVG-ready construction.** A single 9-unit round-capped stroke begins at (47,13), runs left to the first bend, turns through an S-shaped sequence, and ends at (16,49). Overlay the first 14 vertical units in amber. Preserve a minimum 7-unit internal gap around turns so the S is legible at 16 px.

## Direction 3 — Evidence Window

![Evidence Window](./assets/logo-directions/evidence-window.svg)

**Idea.** Three equal windows represent evidence held across time. The small highlighted inset identifies a current, reviewable state rather than claiming an answer is legally correct.

**Palette.** Slate `#25364D`; current teal `#11A683`; monochrome `#25364D` or white.

**Type.** Use Inter, 650 weight, lower-case wordmark with 0.5% tracking. It is broadly available in product and web environments and remains clear at small sizes.

**SVG-ready construction.** Three 36 × 12 rectangular outlines sit at x=14 and y=10, 26, 42, each with a 5-unit stroke. A 12 × 6 teal inset sits at (35,29), inside the centre window. Keep the outline square rather than rounded so it retains an archival, record-like character.

## Recommendation and clearance note

Choose **Verified Thread** for first user testing: it carries the strongest link to Solomon’s verification and dependency model, retains a clear two-bar silhouette at favicon size, and supports a compact app icon. Do not claim exclusivity or use any direction as a final brand until a jurisdiction-appropriate trademark and visual-similarity search is completed. Web research located other unrelated products using the name “Solomon,” including a tax-AI product; a name/logo clearance is therefore required before launch.
