# European Union boundary profile

The `eu` profile applies the common baseline plus GDPR-special-category and MAR/inside-information strict terms. It
routes MAR Article 7 and inside-information terms as MNPI. It is a lexical boundary profile, not a legal decision.

## Research

- [EU AI Act legal-decision-support note](../uk/eu-ai-act.md)

## Citation convention

Prefer current EUR-Lex consolidated text and link to the precise regulation, article, annex, and version date. Record
the operator role, intended purpose, territory where output is used, and any subsequent Commission material separately.

## Known limits

The profile cannot classify an AI system under the AI Act, determine provider/deployer status, establish GDPR/MAR
compliance, or replace Member-State and deployment-specific analysis.
